My story

Let’s start from the beginning. You must have seen him in class — the student who sits in the second or third row. Not the topper, not the backbencher. Just average. A classic jack of all trades, master of none.

Then that guy found a spark, and decided to flip the table. Yup, that’s me.

I was born and raised in a small town in Gujarat, where childhood was a little bit of everything — sports in the evening, a stage at the cultural event, a textbook at night. I never picked one lane; I wanted to try them all. After the 10th, I made up my mind: I wanted to be an engineer.

During high school, I developed an interest in physics and maths, but the stars didn’t align to get my preferred branch (Engineering Physics). Later, I joined Civil Engineering at IIT Tirupati. Not what I had planned, but life rarely follows the plan. Nevertheless, I started liking this also — it turns out curiosity travels with me. Soon, I joined IIT Bombay to pursue my master’s in Structural Engineering. That spark of love towards physics and maths hasn’t blown out yet. My primary interest now lies in computational mechanics.

As a structural engineer, I don’t box myself into one domain. I’m stretching towards aerospace and mechanical systems too — after all, a jack of all trades master of none but better than a master of one. A good structural engineer isn’t tied to one domain, but understands how any structure behaves.

That’s the story so far. If you’d like to talk, or just say hello, I’d love to hear yours too.

— Arth
